Accessibility

Own My Budget is for everyone. We want every page to be easy to use, whatever device or assistive technology you use.

What we do

  • Pages work with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Buttons, menus, accordions and dialogs announce their state to screen readers.
  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and buttons are designed for readable contrast, and layouts adapt from phones to large screens.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are turned off.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and we keep improving.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, please let us know so we can fix it. Our support email is being set up and will be listed here before launch.
