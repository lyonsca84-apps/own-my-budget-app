Debt snowball

Store card1st · $300
Credit card$1,200
Car loan$2,500
Example debts

Make minimum payments on all debts, then put extra money toward the smallest balance first. After it is paid off, move to the next smallest balance.

Focus: smaller balances first.

Debt avalanche

Credit card1st · 24% APR
Store card18% APR
Car loan9% APR
Example debts

Make minimum payments on all debts, then put extra money toward the debt with the highest interest rate first. After it is paid off, move to the next highest rate.

Focus: higher interest rates first.

Compare the approaches to understand the tradeoffs. Estimates depend on the balances, rates, payments, and assumptions entered.
