About Own My Budget

Make your money make sense.

Simple education, practical tools, and step-by-step support for real life.

Own My Budget was created to make budgeting feel clearer, calmer, and more useful for everyday life. We help people organize income, bills, debt, savings, and goals in one place, with education and guidance built into the experience. Our tools are made for real households, including people living paycheck to paycheck, managing changing income, or planning on a fixed income. We believe everyone deserves to understand their money and feel confident taking the next step.

Our story

Own My Budget is an educational budgeting app from Lyons Financial Care. It helps everyday people organize their finances, understand their choices, and build confidence through practical tools and step-by-step guidance.

Our mission: Own My Budget helps people living on paychecks, fixed incomes, or changing income understand their money, plan for real life, and build confidence through simple education, practical tools, and step-by-step support.

Our vision: We envision a future where everyday people can make informed money decisions without shame, confusion, or needing a financial background.

Who it's for

Own My Budget is designed for people age 14 and older who want practical help with money. That includes hourly workers, households living paycheck to paycheck, people on fixed incomes, retirees, single parents, first-time budgeters, and anyone whose income or expenses change from month to month.

They are not looking for a lecture. They want to know whether the bills are covered, what can be spent safely, how to make progress on debt or savings, and what small action would help next.

What we value

  • Clarity. We organize money information so you can understand it quickly and decide what to do next.
  • Compassion. We never shame people for their income, debt, spending, or starting point.
  • Progress. Steady improvement matters more than a perfect budget. A small completed step counts.
  • Practicality. We design for real pay schedules, real bills, changing expenses, and busy lives.
  • Ownership. You stay in control of your money and your decisions.
  • Trust. We explain how information is used, protect personal data, and avoid promises we cannot support.
