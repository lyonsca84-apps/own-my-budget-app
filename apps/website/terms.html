An educational tool

Own My Budget provides educational information and budgeting tools. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, legal or credit advice, and it is not a bank. It does not hold, move or manage money. You remain responsible for your financial decisions.

Plans and billing

The Free plan costs nothing. Guided and Budget Buddy are subscriptions billed monthly or yearly through Stripe at the prices shown on the pricing page. Budget Buddy includes a free trial; you won't be charged if you cancel before it ends. You can cancel or change your plan any time from My account.

Fair use

AI and photo tools include usage allowances for each plan. Allowances are shown on the pricing page and in My account.
