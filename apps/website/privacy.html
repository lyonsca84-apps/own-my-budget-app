Privacy

How Own My Budget handles your information, in plain language.

Draft for review. This page is a plain-language summary while the full Privacy Policy is being finalized. It is not yet a reviewed legal document.

What we collect

Your email address and password to create your account, and the budgeting information you choose to enter, such as income, bills, debts, savings goals and transactions.

How it is protected

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Access rules are set so each account can only read its own information.

Payments

Paid plans are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe. Own My Budget does not see or store your full card number.

Budget Buddy and photo tools

When you ask Budget Buddy a question or scan a receipt or pantry photo, that content is sent to an AI service provider to create a response. Budget Buddy does not move money.

Your choices

You can export your data or permanently delete your account from the app's settings under Data & privacy.
